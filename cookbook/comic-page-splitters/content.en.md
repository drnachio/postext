# One page, three plans

## The Porthcove Light · page 12 · layout proof

Three plans for the same page, for the author to choose from. Each one holds the same five pictures and the same lines of dialogue; only the split of the page changes, and the crops and the balloons follow it.

**Plan A, page 2.** Three tiers, the middle one cut into three panels.

:::paragraphs{style="split" dir=ltr}
split="30 / 35 [\* | \* | \*] / \*"
:::

**Plan B, page 3.** Two columns, the left one in two panels, the right one in three.

:::paragraphs{style="split" dir=ltr}
split="60 [\* / \*] | \* [\* / \* / \*]"
:::

**Plan C, page 4.** A tier 30% deep, cut 30 | 20 | the rest, over one tall panel; the cat is an inset over Maya’s panel.

:::paragraphs{style="split" dir=ltr}
split="30 [30 | 20 | \*] / \*"
:::

Story and lettering for the Postext Cookbook. Pictures generated with diffusion models. Lettered in Comic Neue and Bangers (SIL OFL).
