:::exercise{type="open" lines="6"}
A box of colouring pencils costs £3.75. How much do four boxes cost? If you pay with a £20 note, how much change do you get?

:::solution
Four boxes cost £3.75 × 4 = £15. Your change is £20 − £15 = £5.
:::
