# Winter {months="December · January · February" lead="Below fourteen degrees the bees draw together into a ball on the comb and live on their autumn stores."}

The cluster in :ref{id="winter-frame"} is how the colony gets through the cold. The bees on the outside pack tight, heads in, and hold the heat in; those inside shiver their flight muscles to keep the core warm, and the whole ball moves slowly up the frames as it eats. Do not open the hive. Once a month, heft it from the back to feel the weight and clear the dead bees from the entrance.
