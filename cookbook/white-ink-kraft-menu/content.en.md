# Since 1987 {style="back"}

:::paragraphs{style="story"}
The oven in the back room was built by Rosa Alto’s father the year the bakery opened, from brick and the clay of the river below the village. It still bakes every loaf. Rosa runs the bakery now with her son Pablo, who came back from three years in a bakery in Lyon with a croissant he would not stop talking about.

We bake with flour from two mills in the province, butter from Asturias and eggs from the farm on the Sigüenza road. What is not sold by seven goes to the shelter on Calle Mayor.
:::
