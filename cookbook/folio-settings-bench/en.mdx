## What you'll build

*Six Knots*, a ten-page pocket book on a 110 × 160 mm trim, opened in Folio next to a panel with every setting of `config.folio`. Pick a stock and the leaves change colour, surface and stiffness; switch the binding from paperback to hardcover and a case grows round the block; choose the case instead of the printed covers, a cloth or leather material, a desk, a light, a tilt. The book is laid out once. Every change goes to the viewer with `setAppearance`, so no line moves and no page is painted again, and the settings appear under the form as the code to paste into `config.folio`.

## Method

### 1 · Name the fields after the config

<Excerpt region="form" />

Each field of the form is named by its path in `config.folio` (`paper.grammage`, `binding.cover`, `lighting.environment`), so one loop turns the form into the object the viewer takes: numbers from the sliders, booleans from the checkboxes, a `{ hex, model }` colour from the picker. The same object is the document's own `config.folio` when the pen builds it, so the Sandbox opens the book as the form starts.

### 2 · Covers the viewer can turn as boards

<Excerpt region="covers" />

With `binding.cover: 'pages'` the first page is the front board and the last the back, when it falls on a verso. The back cover's heading style breaks to an even page, so the book always ends on a verso and both covers turn. Switch the form to *A case round the pages* and the same two pages turn as ordinary leaves inside a cloth case.

### 3 · Restyle the book in place

The code is [the short answer](#the-short-answer) above. `setAppearance({ folio })` replaces the document's settings and redraws the scene: paper thickness and stiffness, the case, the desk maps and the light. The pages keep their paintings, so a change lands in the next frame. A stock sets its own weight, which is why picking one moves the weight slider; `resetView()` brings the camera back after the reader orbits the book with a right-drag.

## Variations

### Start from a hardcover in a cloth case

Change the form's defaults in `index.html`; the document and the Sandbox follow them.

```diff
-        <option value="hardcover">Hardcover</option><option value="paperback" selected>Paperback</option>
+        <option value="hardcover" selected>Hardcover</option><option value="paperback">Paperback</option>
```
