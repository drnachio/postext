# Lettering style sheet

## The Porthcove Light · issue 3 · page 7

One line of the script is one balloon: the speaker, the kind in braces when it is not plain speech, a colon, the words. The shapes below are the ones on page 2.

- **Speech.** :chip[tomas: Make it two.]{style="code"} An oval; the tail stops short of the speaker’s mouth.
- **Joined.** Two :chip[tomas:]{style="code"} lines in a row butt into one outline, with one tail.
- **Thought.** :chip[biscuit{thought}:]{style="code"} A cloud, with bubbles that rise towards the head.
- **Whisper.** :chip[maya{whisper}:]{style="code"} A dashed outline and smaller letters.
- **Shout.** :chip[tomas{shout}:]{style="code"} A burst, in bold, a size up.
- **Radio.** The skipper speaks through the set: the cast gives every :chip[skipper:]{style="code"} line a jagged outline and a zigzag tail.
- **Caption.** :chip[caption:]{style="code"} Narration in a yellow box, butted into a corner.
- **Inner voice.** :chip[maya{inner}:]{style="code"} What she does not say aloud: no tail, a grey box, italics.
- **Editor’s note.** :chip[note:]{style="code"} Small, in the panel’s lower corner.
- **Sound effect.** :chip[sfx{at="16% 28%" rotate=-10}:]{style="code"} No balloon: display letters in red with a white outline, placed and turned by hand.
- **Off-panel.** :chip[tomas{tail=end}:]{style="code"} The speaker is outside the picture; the tail runs to the panel’s edge.
- **Pinned.** :chip[skipper{at="44% 22%"}:]{style="code"} The balloon is centred on that point of the picture, whatever the crop.
