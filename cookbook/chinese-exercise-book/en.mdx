## What you'll build

Two units of a second-year 练习册 on a 16开 page: a language unit, 春天来了, and a maths unit on sums up to 100. Each opens on a watercolour band, a subject tab and a line for class, name and score, then runs through the sections a Chinese workbook prints: 看拼音写词语, readings to ring, 选择题 with a （　） after the stem and A. B. C. D. in a row, 判断题 with a bracket at the end of the line, gaps, 连线 between measure words and things, a sentence to write on 田字格, 口算 in four columns, 竖式 with a box for each digit and a word problem. The points sit in each instruction, as the pupil reads them. The same Markdown is laid out twice: the pupil's book, with 参考答案 at the end of each unit, and the teacher's book, with every answer written in red Kai in its place.

## Method

### 1 · One style for every section

The code is [the short answer](#the-short-answer) above. Sections are counted in Chinese numerals with a full-width 、 after them, so no word space follows the number; the instruction is set in the Hei face, unbolded, so the points in brackets read as part of it. `options.bracket` writes the （　） after each stem, and the right letter goes into it in the teacher's book. The two small styles extend the main one: `pinyin` draws each blank as a box as wide as its word and prints the reading of `[[{春天|chūn tiān}]]` over it, in the pupil's book too; `kuohao` draws nothing, because the bracket of 在（　）里填 is in the text.

### 2 · The pupil's book and the teacher's from one text

<Excerpt region="editions" />

The configuration is a function, so each build gets its own object and the Sandbox bundle keeps the pupil's one. `edition: 'teacher'` writes the answers in the colour and face of `answers`: the words in their boxes, the letters in their brackets, √ and × in the 判断题, the digits in the 竖式 cells, the model sentence one character to a square. The pupil's book keeps the key for the `:::solutions` line at the end of each unit; the teacher's book needs none.

### 3 · A unit opener with its painting

<Excerpt region="opener" />

The painting is anchored to the page, so it runs to the top edge on a recto and on a verso alike; the subject tab and the title are placed from the top of the text block, below the painting's foot. Both units share the design and differ in their heading attributes, `kicker` and `pic`.

### 4 · Folios at the foot

<Excerpt region="furniture" />

Chinese workbooks put the folio at the foot. The series title comes from the Markdown front matter (`{title}`), the unit's from its heading (`{chapterTitle}`).

## Variations

### Print the key at the end of the chapter

Without `:::solutions` lines, `chapterEnd` collects each unit's key before the next level-1 heading.

```diff
-  solutions: { placement: edition === 'teacher' ? 'none' : 'directive' } });
+  solutions: { placement: edition === 'teacher' ? 'none' : 'chapterEnd' } });
```

### Give each word its 田字格

An open question with `cells="2 2 2 2"` sets a group of squares for each word under a line of pinyin, as <RecipeLink slug="school-traditions-worksheet">the Chinese edition of the ten-tradition worksheet</RecipeLink> ends.

## Pitfalls

- The Kai face sets only the answers, so it loads the characters of the gaps and the model answers alone; a character it sets anywhere else must be added to that text.
