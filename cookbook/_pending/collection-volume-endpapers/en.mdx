## What you'll build

*The Deceitful Marriage*, the eleventh of Cervantes' twelve *Exemplary Novels*, as volume XI of a series that prints one novella per hardcover. The 26 pages are set in Crimson Pro 11/15.2 on a 120 × 185 mm page. `buildCover` then makes three sheets for the binder from them: the printed case, in the series' oxblood with a gold frame round the painting and the number in a gold ring, and the front and back endpapers, which show the twelve volumes as miniatures of that case above a numbered list of titles. Beside the barcode, which carries the volume's ISBN, the case prints the ISBN of the complete work.

## Method

### 1 · The collection is data of the book

The code is [the short answer](#the-short-answer) above. `production.collection` holds the series: its name, its ISBN and a `catalogue` with an id, a title and a picture for each volume. The version's `workIsbn` is what Postext prints: a line in black 6.5 pt over the barcode, "ISBN of the complete work", in the book's language. The catalogue travels with the book in the `.postext` file, and the configuration checks read it, but `buildCover` draws nothing from it, so the pen lays it out itself in step 3. Both ISBNs are sample numbers in the 978-84-0000 block, assigned to no book.

### 2 · One livery for every volume

<Excerpt region="livery" />

The design of the case is the same object on every volume of the series; only `VOLUME`, the painting and the texts change. Each item is placed by the zone it stands in, so when another volume has more pages and a thicker spine the front moves with its panel. On the spine, `basis` records the 10 mm spine the bands were drawn on, and a spine of 8.3 mm or 14 mm stretches the bands to its width and keeps the badge on its centre line. The caption over the barcode is set in black, so the barcode sits on a pale label.

### 3 · The endpapers are laid out from the catalogue

<Excerpt region="endpapers" />

An item whose zone a sheet does not have is left out of that sheet. The miniatures stand in `pastedown` and `flyleaf`, so they appear on the endpapers and not on the case, and the painting on `front` stays off the endpapers. The text works the same way: one cover Markdown serves every sheet, the back cover's frame is dropped on the endpapers and the endpapers' frames on the case. The pen writes the endpaper frames from the catalogue, so the list and the miniatures cannot disagree. Both endpapers take the same design and text, and at the back of the book the pastedown sits on the right: volumes VII–XII come first there.

### 4 · Three sheets from one book

<Excerpt region="sheets" />

The same call with `sheet: 'cover'`, `'endpaperFront'` and `'endpaperBack'` makes the case and the two endpapers, each a document of one page at its own size. The 26 pages round up to two 16-page signatures, 16 leaves of 240 µm; with 0.5 mm of allowance the block is 4.3 mm thick and the case spine 8.3 mm, two boards of 2 mm added. The checks list `spineTooThinForText` for the 4.3 mm block, while the lettering goes on the 8.3 mm spine of the case, which is wider than the 6 mm minimum. The PDF holds the 26 pages, then the case and the endpapers.

## Variations

### Wrap the case in a dust jacket

On a case, `flaps` adds a jacket, a fourth sheet with flaps of its own, and its back cover carries the barcode and the work's ISBN as the case does.

```diff
   versions: [{ id: 'hardcover', name: t({ en: 'Hardcover', es: 'Tapa dura' }),
     binding: 'case-flat', isbn: '978-84-0000-198-8', isbnAddOn: '90000',
-    workIsbn: collection.isbn }],
+    workIsbn: collection.isbn, flaps: true }],
```

### Set the next volume

For volume XII the design stays as it is: the number, the gold frame on the endpapers and the highlighted title follow `VOLUME`. The text, the painting and the ISBN are that volume's.

```diff
-const VOLUME = 11; // this book's place in the collection
+const VOLUME = 12; // this book's place in the collection
```

## Pitfalls

- The sheets come after the book's pages in the captured list: the case is page 27, the book's count plus one. A change that adds a page to the book moves them.
- The Cookbook's `contentInConfig` check counts the line `buildCover` prints over the barcode as text kept in a design. The pen lists it in `checks.contentInConfig.allow`, worded by `workIsbnCaption`, the function the engine uses.
- The barcode's digits name OCR-B, which no font server has. Load the barcode with a `font-family` the pen already loads, or the PDF fails.
