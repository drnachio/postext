Exemplary Novels · XI
:::

:::paragraphs{style="back-blurb"}
An ensign leaves the hospital of Valladolid leaning on his sword. Over dinner he tells his friend Peralta how he married a lady with a furnished house and a chest of linen, and how both turned out to belong to someone else.
:::

:::paragraphs{style="back-blurb"}
At the end of his story he hands Peralta a roll of paper: two nights of talk he says he overheard between two dogs of the hospital, which is the twelfth and last volume of this collection.
:::
