:::paragraphs{style="imprint"}
The fables follow George Fyler Townsend’s translation from the Greek, *Three Hundred Æsop’s Fables* (London: George Routledge and Sons, 1867), as transcribed by Project Gutenberg (eBook #21). The text is in the public domain; the opening words of each fable, set in capitals there, are set in lower case here.

Set in Libre Caslon Text, Libre Caslon Display and Jost (SIL Open Font License). Laid out once with Postext and written out as two EPUB 3 files, one with fixed pages and one with reflowable text.
:::
