## What you'll build

The first chapter of *The Ferryman’s Ledger*, a short novel on a 129 × 198 mm page, opened as a book on a walnut desk. The case is bottle-green cloth, the pages are a bulky cream novel paper, and the reader takes the right-hand page by its edge and turns it, the leaf curling and casting its shadow on the page below. The text is set once with `buildDocument`; one call to the `postext-folio` package turns that layout into the book. Everything the book is made of (paper, binding, desk, light, the angle of the view) is part of the config, so the same document carries its look into the Sandbox’s Folio tab.

## Method

### 1 · Say what the book is made of

<Excerpt region="folio" />

`config.folio` is read by the 3D viewer and by nothing else: the layout, the canvas, the PDF and the HTML output ignore it, so changing the paper never moves a line. `bookWove` is a bulky novel paper, and 70 g/m² at its bulk makes a sheet about 112 µm thick; the viewer multiplies that by the page count for the depth of the page block. The cloth colour links to the palette's accent, the same green as the kicker and the folios.

### 2 · A quiet opener and running heads

<Excerpt region="opener" />

The chapter opens a third of the way down a recto, under a kicker taken from the heading's attributes. In 3D a sunk opener reads as it does in a printed novel: the white space at the head of the page is the first thing the reader sees when the case is opened.

<Excerpt region="running-heads" />

### 3 · Open the document as a book

The code is [the short answer](#the-short-answer) above. `createFolioFromDocument` takes a container and the laid-out document: it paints each page with `renderPageToCanvas` at the exact size of a page slot, only around the open spread, and fits the book into the container. The container needs a height of its own, or the book has no room. `textureBaseUrl` points at the photographed desks on postext.dev; without it the walnut is drawn with procedural maps.

## Variations

### A paperback on oak in daylight

Change three settings and the same chapter is a softcover on a pale desk; nothing is laid out again.

```diff
-  binding: { type: 'hardcover', cover: 'case', coverMaterial: 'cloth',
-    coverColor: col('accent') }, // the case wraps the pages; boards a little larger
-  surface: { type: 'walnut' },
-  lighting: { environment: 'studio', intensity: 1, shadows: true },
+  binding: { type: 'paperback', cover: 'case', coverMaterial: 'paper',
+    coverColor: col('accent') },
+  surface: { type: 'oak' },
+  lighting: { environment: 'daylight', intensity: 1, shadows: true },
```
