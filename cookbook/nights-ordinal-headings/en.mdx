## What you'll build

Nine pages from the beginning of the *Thousand and One Nights*: a half-title, then the Merchant and the Jinni, the first tale Shahrazad tells, which runs over three nights. Each night opens with Hindawi’s formula فلما كانت in small Kufi over its number in green Ruqʿa, الليلة الأولى, الليلة الثانية, الليلة الثالثة, written out as the books print it. None of those numbers is in the Markdown: the heading level counts the nights and writes each count as a feminine ordinal, which agrees with ليلة. The tale opens under a gold-ruled headpiece, the three old men’s stories have their own small titles, and the running heads carry the book and the tale. The text is the Hindawi Foundation’s 2022 edition, under CC BY 4.0.

## Method

### 1 · Nights counted in words

The code is [the short answer](#the-short-answer) above. A night is a level-2 heading, `## فلما كانت`, and the level’s `numberingTemplate` is `'الليلة {2:ordinal-feminine}'`: the `-feminine` modifier writes الأولى, الثانية, الحادية عشرة, الحادية بعد الألف ([Ordinal and cardinal words](/en/docs/arabic-layout#ordinal-and-cardinal-words), [Spelled-out numbers](/en/docs/configuration#spelled-out-numbers)). The design prints `{number}` large and the heading’s own text, Hindawi’s فلما كانت, small above it. The tale headings use a style with `numbered: false`: a numbered level 1 would restart the night count with every tale, while an unnumbered one leaves the deeper counters alone, so the nights run on through the book.

### 2 · A headpiece for each tale

<Excerpt region="opener" />

The level-1 heading is a band of tint between two gold rules, the tale’s title in Ruqʿa over it, a kicker and a Latin line from heading attributes, `{kicker="…" latin="…"}`. The Latin line is the only part of the opener that changes with the edition.

### 3 · Running heads that come with the tale

<Excerpt region="heads" />

The running heads belong to the `tale` heading style, so each tale brings its own; the half-title has none. The verso carries the book’s title and the recto `{chapterTitle}`, the tale. In a book bound on the right the verso is the right-hand page, so its folio goes to the top right ([Page order and right binding](/en/docs/arabic-layout#page-order-and-right-binding)).

### 4 · A half-title in Ruqʿa

<Excerpt region="title" />

The half-title is a heading style with `span: 'page'`, `numbered: false` and empty header and footer, so it neither counts nor carries a running head.

## Variations

### Start at the thousand and first night

`{startAt=1001}` on a night heading sets its counter, and the template writes الليلة الحادية بعد الألف ([Heading attributes](/en/docs/document-format#heading-attributes)).

```diff
-## فلما كانت
+## فلما كانت {startAt=1001}
```

### Spell the hundreds the Bulaq way

`-classical` writes مائة for مئة, so the two hundredth night reads الليلة المائتان.

```diff
-  level: 2, numberingTemplate: 'الليلة {2:ordinal-feminine}',
+  level: 2, numberingTemplate: 'الليلة {2:ordinal-feminine-classical}',
```

### Number chapters in the masculine

Without a modifier the ordinal is masculine, for a noun such as الفصل: الفصل الأول، الفصل الثاني.

```diff
-  level: 2, numberingTemplate: 'الليلة {2:ordinal-feminine}',
+  level: 2, numberingTemplate: 'الفصل {2:ordinal}',
```
