Don Quixote in His Library
Part I, ch. I: ‘which treats of the character and pursuits of the famous gentleman Don Quixote of La Mancha’
Don Quixote sits upright in a carved chair, a book in his left hand and a sword raised in his right, surrounded by the figures of his reading: knights on horseback, a dragon, a giant’s severed head, a lady in chains and two tiny knights jousting on mice.

The Vigil of Arms
Part I, ch. III: ‘wherein is related the droll way in which Don Quixote had himself dubbed a knight’
A moonlit inn yard. Don Quixote, bareheaded and holding a lance and a buckler, looks up at the moon; his armour stands propped on the well-head facing him, and a figure watches from the inn’s outside stair.

The Adventure of the Windmills
Part I, ch. VIII: ‘the terrible and undreamt-of adventure of the windmills’
A windmill’s sail sweeps Don Quixote and Rocinante off the ground, the knight tangled against the canvas and his buckler flung out; a row of mills stands on the horizon and Sancho, hatless, throws up one arm and clutches his head beside his ass.
