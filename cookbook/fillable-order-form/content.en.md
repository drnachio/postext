# Seed List and Order Form {style="cover" kicker="Spring 2027 · Open-pollinated seed" standfirst="Twenty-five varieties grown out on our own fields last summer, cleaned by hand and tested for germination in October."}

:::columns{count=2}
This sheet is our price list and your order in one. Open it in a PDF reader and fill it in on screen: the totals add themselves up as you type the number of packets, and you can save the file and send it to us by email. Or print it, write in the boxes and post it with your payment.

Every variety on the list is open-pollinated. Save the seed of the best plants in a row and it comes true the next year, which the seed of a hybrid will not do. Some of these strains have been kept by one family for a century; others came to us from gardeners who sent a few seeds in an envelope with a note on where they came from. We grow each one at least a quarter of a mile from anything it could cross with, and we test every lot in October: a lot that germinates below 80 per cent never leaves the barn.

We pack orders on Tuesdays and Fridays from January to May, and most reach you within a week. Keep the packets somewhere cool and dry until you sow them, in their paper envelopes rather than in a jar on a warm kitchen shelf.
:::

Make checks payable to Long Furrow Heritage Seeds and post them with the form. A filled-in file sent by email is answered within two days with the details for paying by card over the phone.

If a packet fails to come up for you this season, write and tell us what happened and we will send another, or the price back, as you prefer. Seed that fails in one garden often thrives in the next, and your note helps us learn which.

Members of the seed savers' circle get the list of the August swap, where they trade the seed they have saved with one another and with us. Every variety on this sheet came to Long Furrow that way, or from a gift to the seed library.
:::
