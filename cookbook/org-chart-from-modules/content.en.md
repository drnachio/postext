# How your town hall is run

Valdeloma has 8,400 residents and a town hall with four departments. Each department answers to a councillor and gathers the services it runs: if you do not know whom to ask, find the service here and call its department.

# Where to find us

The council's services fill six buildings, all less than ten minutes' walk from the Plaza Mayor. Every building is free to enter; courses and court hire are paid at the residents' office on the ground floor of the town hall.

:::module{template="aviso"}
:::frame{slot="texto" label="Residents' office"}
Ground floor of the town hall, 1 Plaza Mayor. Monday to Friday, 8.30 am to 2.30 pm, and on Thursdays also 5 pm to 7 pm. This is where you ask for certificates, pay for courses and courts, and hand in a complaint or a suggestion about any service. **Phone 010**, from any landline or mobile in the town.
