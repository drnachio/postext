## What you'll build

Two pages from a third-year drill series on B5. The arithmetic page runs across: a pale blue band with the subject badge, the title and a tanuki mascot, a name line and a score box, then ひっ算 with a box for each digit of the answer, number boxes after six sums, ア イ ウ choices in a row, ○× statements with a bracket at the end of the line and two word problems with lines to write the working on. The kanji page is set the way a 国語 drill is printed, top to bottom and right to left: readings with a box for the kanji, lined words whose reading goes in brackets, choices to ring and a meaning to pick. Under every exercise, its answers are printed upside down in small grey type. A child sees where they are, cannot read them at a glance, and turns the book round to check.

## Method

### 1 · Turn each exercise's key upside down

The code is [the short answer](#the-short-answer) above. `placement: 'inverted'` sets the key after each exercise in a box turned 180°, and the box goes inside the exercise's own box, so a page break never parts the two. The key is made from the exercise: the operations with their results, the katakana of the right option, ○ and ×, the words of the gaps. The answers box style, `kotae`, only sets the type small and grey.

### 2 · One band for a page across and a page down

<Excerpt region="header" />

A heading design on a vertical page is laid along its column: x runs down the page and y runs to the left. The band anchored `top-left` with the full width stands at the top of the arithmetic page and down the right edge of the kanji page; the mascot anchored `top-right` sits at the band's far end on both. Nothing in the design names a direction.

### 3 · Two drills from one text

<Excerpt region="two-books" />

The configuration is a function, so each build gets its own object, and only `writingMode` changes between the two. Set vertically, the 国語 drill binds on the right by itself; `showBook` lays each drill out on its own side of the spine. The text is set in Klee One, a textbook face, and the labels in Zen Maru Gothic; both load their vertical punctuation for the brackets of the kanji page. On the kanji page each `[[{春|はる}]]` prints its reading right of its box, where furigana stand beside a vertical line; the `kaki` style narrows the box to one kanji.

## Variations

### Collect the answers at the end

A drill printed with a separate answer booklet keeps the key out of the pages: `chapterEnd` lists it after the last exercise.

```diff
-  solutions: { placement: 'inverted', box: 'kotae' },
+  solutions: { placement: 'chapterEnd', box: 'kotae' },
```
