Chapter 1 · The foundations of biochemistry
:::
:::paragraphs{style="cover-blurb"}
This first chapter gathers the basic notions needed to make sense of a subject such as biochemistry. Every reader beginning a course of this kind will most likely have studied all of these concepts already; they may, however, have grown a little hazy or been half forgotten. This chapter sets out to clarify them and to place in a biological context the concepts and foundations a correct understanding of biochemistry — and of its application to the health sciences — depends on.

Many of the points described in this first chapter are taken up again in more depth later in the book, and in some cases the reader may want to come back here to look something up or go over it again.
