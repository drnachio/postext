# About this edition {style="preliminar" lead="The National Atlas of Spain is a work of the Instituto Geográfico Nacional that, since 1955, has gathered a complete description of the Spanish territory in maps, charts and texts."}

Its latest edition, *Spain in maps. A geographical synthesis*, is published chapter by chapter in digital form under an open licence, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, which allows both texts and maps to be reproduced and reused with credit.

This sample edition re-sets the chapter of historical references, a journey from the Paleolithic to democracy, with the maps the IGN drew for each era. The setting is new: a 260 × 365 mm page in two columns, page-wide and column maps with their captions, section openers with their own palette and a contents page generated by Postext. Texts and maps are the atlas's own, unchanged.
