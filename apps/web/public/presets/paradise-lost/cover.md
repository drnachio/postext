The poem
:::
:::paragraphs{style="cover-flap"}
*Paradise Lost* was first printed in ten books in 1667 and in twelve in 1674, the year of Milton’s death. This edition sets the poem of 1674 line for line, in modern spelling, with the standard numbering of the lines.

Each book opens with the Argument Milton wrote for it, a prose summary of what is to come.

This edition
:::
:::paragraphs{style="cover-flap"}
The notes are a selection from A. W. Verity’s edition (Cambridge, 1910), keyed to the lines. The plates are Gustave Doré’s wood engravings for the Cassell edition of 1866.

Set in EB Garamond and the Fell types digitised by Igino Marini. A showcase edition of Postext, postext.dev.
