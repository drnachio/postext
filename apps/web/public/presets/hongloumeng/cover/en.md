The author
:::
:::paragraphs{style="cover-flap"}
Cao Xueqin (c. 1715–1763) came of a family that served the Qing emperors as textile commissioners in Nanjing for three generations and lost its fortune when he was a boy. He wrote the first eighty chapters of the novel in poverty near Beijing; the last forty were completed by Cheng Weiyuan and Gao E, who printed the whole in 1791 and 1792.
